## Shipping and delivery
Orders within Lagos are usually delivered in 1-3 working days; other states in 3-7 working days.
You get a tracking update when the vendor hands the parcel to the courier.

## Returns and refunds
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused, with tags and original packaging.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-10 working days after the vendor receives the item.
Underwear, swimwear and customised items cannot be returned.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order until the vendor ships it. After shipping, request a return instead.

## Payments
We accept cards, bank transfer and USSD. We never ask for your card PIN or OTP by chat or phone.

## Vendor payouts
Vendors are paid every Friday for orders delivered and not returned in the previous week.
Payouts go to the bank account in your vendor settings.

## Choosing a size
Each product page has a size chart from the vendor. If you're between sizes, pick the larger one for shoes.
